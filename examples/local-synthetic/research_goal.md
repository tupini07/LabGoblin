# Research question

Does adding the same offset to the three generated values change their mean by
exactly that offset, while identical settings reproduce the baseline?

Use the dependency-free `experiment.py`. Establish the zero-offset baseline,
repeat it, then compare an offset of one. Preserve the output files, record
reproducible commands, and explain the result and the limits of this tiny
deterministic demonstration in `report.md`. Do not turn it into a broader
benchmark or install dependencies.
